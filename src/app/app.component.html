📊 About Financial Year & Tax Calculation

  • Financial Year: April 1st to March 31st of the next year
  • Exchange Rate: Uses CBSL buying rate for salary date
  • Taxing Month: Salary received in a month is taxed in the same month
  • Tax Calculation: Progressive tax system based on annual salary
  • Data Storage: All data is saved locally in your browser

📊 Data Sources

This application tries multiple sources to get you the most accurate exchange rates:

  1. Primary: CBSL (Central Bank of Sri Lanka) - Official rates
  2. Backup: ExchangeRate-API - Real-time market rates
  3. Fallback: Static reference data when online sources are unavailable

⚠️ When official CBSL data is not available (weekends/holidays), estimated rates from backup sources are used.
